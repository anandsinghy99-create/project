About Campus Event Portal

Welcome to the official Campus Event Management & Registration Portal. Our platform is designed to bridge the gap between university management and students by bringing all technical, cultural, and sports events under one roof.

Students can easily explore schedules, view venue details, and participate in campus activities without missing out on important updates or facing paper-work hassles.

Why Choose Our Portal?

All university events, venues, and schedules listed neatly.
